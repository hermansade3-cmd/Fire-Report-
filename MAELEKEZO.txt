MAELEKEZO — Kuongeza Incident AI + Offline (PWA) kwenye website uliyonayo
==========================================================================

HATUA 1 — Pakia faili hizi 5 kwenye folda moja na index.html yako
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - incident-ai.js
  - manifest.json
  - sw.js
  - icon-192.png
  - icon-512.png

(Usiguse index.html, style.css, script.js, core.js, sync.js, announcements.js,
au APK yako — zote zinabaki kama zilivyo.)


HATUA 2 — Fungua index.html yako, ongeza mstari 1 ndani ya <head>
------------------------------------------------------------------
Tafuta mstari wenye:
  <link rel="stylesheet" href="style.css...">

Kabla yake (au baada yake), ongeza mstari huu:
  <link rel="manifest" href="manifest.json">


HATUA 3 — Ongeza mistari hii kabla ya </body>, BAADA ya script zako zote
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Karibu na mwisho wa index.html, pale ambapo umeweka <script src="announcements.js">
au script ya mwisho, ongeza mistari hii MARA MOJA TU (usiirudie):

  <script src="incident-ai.js"></script>
  <script>
    if ("serviceWorker" in navigator) {
      window.addEventListener("load", () => {
        navigator.serviceWorker.register("sw.js").catch(() => {});
      });
    }
  </script>

Kisha </body></html> kama kawaida.


HATUA 4 (SI LAZIMA) — fire_education.html yako
------------------------------------------------
Kama unataka ukurasa huo nao ufanye kazi bila internet baada ya kufunguliwa
mara moja, ongeza mistari hii kabla ya </script> ya mwisho ya faili hiyo:

  if ('serviceWorker' in navigator) {
    window.addEventListener('load', () => {
      navigator.serviceWorker.register('sw.js').catch(() => {});
    });
  }

Hii si ya lazima kwa Incident AI kufanya kazi — ni kwa ajili ya offline-caching
tu ya ukurasa huo maalum.


HATUA 5 — Angalia sw.js yako mpya (SI LAZIMA kuihariri)
----------------------------------------------------------
Ndani ya sw.js kuna orodha ya majina ya faili (APP_SHELL) inayotarajia majina
yafuatayo: index.html, style.css?v=3, core.js?v=5, script.js?v=6, sync.js?v=1,
announcements.js?v=3. Kama majina/version (?v=) ya faili zako ni tofauti kidogo,
fungua sw.js na urekebishe orodha hiyo ili yalingane na majina halisi ya faili
zako (au uache tu — faili moja kutokuwepo haizuii nyingine kuhifadhiwa, service
worker itaruka tu ile isiyopatikana).


HATUA 6 — Jaribu
-------------------
1. Pakia faili zote kwenye hosting yako (GitHub Pages).
2. Fungua website kwenye kivinjari — utaona kitufe cha 🧠 chini-kulia.
3. Bonyeza, uliza swali kama "PASS Technique ni nini" au "ripoti ngapi mwezi
   huu" — utaona majibu bila internet.

Hilo ndilo lote linalohitajika. Incident AI haihitaji mabadiliko yoyote
kwenye script.js, core.js, sync.js, wala announcements.js zako.
